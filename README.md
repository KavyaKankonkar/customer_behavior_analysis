# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end analysis of 3,900 customer purchases using **Python (pandas)**, **SQL**, and **Power BI**. Covers data cleaning, exploratory analysis, business-question queries, and an interactive dashboard.

## Business Questions Answered

1. How does revenue compare between male and female customers, and how does average spend compare?
2. Which high-spending customers used discounts?
3. Which products have the highest average review ratings?
4. How do Standard and Express shipping compare on average purchase amount?
5. Do subscribers spend more than non-subscribers?
6. Which products have the highest share of discounted purchases?
7. How do customers segment into New, Returning, and Loyal groups?
8. What are the top 3 products in each category?
9. Are repeat buyers (more than 5 purchases) more likely to subscribe?
10. How does revenue break down by age group?

## Key Findings

- Subscribers and non-subscribers have nearly identical average spend (about $59.50 vs. $59.87).
- Repeat buyers subscribe at about the same rate as all customers (roughly 27%).
- Male customers generate more total revenue mainly because they make up a larger share of customers.
- Express shipping orders average about $2 more than Standard orders.
